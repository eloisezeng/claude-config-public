**Measured 2026-09-02.** A fleet-UI deletion wave killed seven background jobs in one second (`~/.claude/daemon.log`: `bg settled <id> (killed)`); all seven were recovered or were non-losses (census: `~/.claude/ops/lanes/accidental-session-deletion-2026-09-02.md`).
What deletion destroys: the process, `~/.claude/jobs/<8-hex>/` (state, timeline, `tmp/` — including every codex scratch worktree parked under it), and the git worktree the seat had entered via `EnterWorktree`.
What survives: the transcript `~/.claude/projects/<cwd-as-dashes>/<uuid>.jsonl`, the rewind snapshots at `~/.claude/file-history/<uuid>/<hash>@v<n>` (mapped by the transcript's `file-history-snapshot` entries), the branch on origin, and `~/.claude/session-state/<uuid>.bg`.
Sometimes the daemon RELAUNCHES a killed job itself within a minute: the record reads `state=running`, `pid=None`, `tmp/` intact, and a NEW process sits in the seat's worktree, idle until something nudges it (measured: one took its first turn 54 minutes later). It is not a phantom, and it is invisible to `ps` (no uuid in argv), to `lsof` cwd (a spare-claimed process keeps the daemon's spare dir as its cwd) and to the last-turn test. The reliable signal is the daemon's own log: a `bg claimed-spare <id> (fleet)` line AFTER the `bg settled <id> (killed)` line means the seat is back, and `ListAgents` then lists its title as a peer. Check both before resuming ANY transcript; resuming over a relaunch makes two seats edit one worktree (measured: two duplicates, stopped before either wrote).

**Why:** the transcript is a complete write log.
Every Edit carries `old_string`/`new_string`, every Write its content, and every Bash command its text — including `python3 - <<'PY'` heredocs that `open(p,'w')` a file, which a regex for `sed|cp|mv` will miss (measured: six such patches, and the replay stayed red until they were found).
A step's `tool_result` also records `is_error` and the output text; a step the seat's own guard REFUSED must NOT be replayed (replaying it doubled a test block), while a script that failed PARTWAY applied its earlier writes identically in both runs — compare the recorded result text with the replay log, not the exit code, which on a `cmd; grep …` line is the grep's.

**How to apply:**
1. Identify the seats from `daemon.log` kill timestamps, then find each transcript by uuid prefix across EVERY project dir — a seat's cwd is where it booted, not the handoff's `cwd=` line. A job of the user's own that died with ZERO turns is re-issued verbatim (plus a context paragraph), not resumed.
2. Confirm the daemon did not relaunch it (`grep <id> ~/.claude/daemon.log` — nothing after the `settled (killed)` line) and that `ListAgents` does not list its title, and that the transcript mtime equals the kill time. Retire any phantom record FIRST: `state=failed`, `detail` naming the kill and the successor, `DO NOT REVIVE` — or a watcher revives the corpse.
3. If the worktree or scratch died: `git worktree add <same path> -b <branch> origin/<branch>`, symlink `node_modules`, copy the newest file-history snapshot of each tracked file, then replay every writing command AFTER the snapshot line in transcript order, skipping mutant/restore pairs and any step whose recorded result `is_error`. Rewrite the dead job's `tmp/` path to a durable scratch dir (`~/.claude/ops/recovery/<id>/tmp`, never your own job tmp) in each command.
4. Prove the rebuilt state by matching the seat's OWN last recorded test totals (grep `Tests ` in its tool results), never by "it is green now".
5. Relaunch from the ORIGINAL cwd, each launch on its OWN line with an explicit `cd <cwd> &&` — a `cd` leaks across a compound line and between calls (measured: one seat booted inside another seat's mid-merge worktree) — then verify `cwd` in the new `state.json`: `claude --bg "<resume brief>" --resume=<uuid> --permission-mode=bypassPermissions --model='opus[1m]'`.
   It keeps the same 8-hex job id ONLY when the daemon fully released it (job dir gone AND never re-claimed as a phantom); a record that existed at any point after the kill yields a NEW id — read the id off the launch output, then retire the old record, append a `revival_…` line to the handoff's `.dispatch`, repoint `~/.claude/ops/dispatches/<lane>` and `SendMessage` the seat its real id and scratch path.
   A resumed 68 MB transcript compacts for minutes first; the daemon labels that "stuck on a startup dialog" while `claude logs` shows a "Compacting conversation" spinner — wait, do not re-dispatch.
6. `SendMessage` cannot address a bg seat by job id; use its `ListAgents` title — a resumed seat is renamed from the RESUME brief, and the row's "says it was <old title> until Nm ago" is how you match it.
